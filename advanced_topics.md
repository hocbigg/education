# Advanced Topics in Education

Having mastered the core undergraduate foundations of education, you can now specialize in advanced subdisciplines, methodologies, and research paradigms. Review the specialization tracks below and select the pathway aligned with your research or professional goals:

- **Learning Sciences and Advanced Instructional Design**: Focuses on cognitive architecture, conceptual change, and the engineering of complex collaborative learning environments.
- **Advanced Psychometrics, Data Analytics, and Program Evaluation**: Focuses on latent trait modeling, educational data mining, predictive analytics, and large-scale intervention evaluation.
- **Educational Leadership, Policy, and Improvement Science**: Focuses on organizational framing, continuous improvement methodologies, and the systemic economics of education policy.
- **Critical Pedagogies, Language, and Inclusive Design**: Focuses on liberatory educational models, multilingual translanguaging repertoires, and neurodiversity-driven universal design.
- **Emerging Technologies, AI in Education, and Digital Literacies**: Focuses on intelligent tutoring systems, human-AI pedagogical partnerships, immersive game-based learning, and algorithmic ethics.

## Advanced Psychometrics, Data Analytics, and Program Evaluation

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item Response Theory and Modern Measurement | Models the mathematical probability of test-item responses as a function of latent examinee traits. It provides the psychometric theoretical backbone required for large-scale standardized testing, computer-adaptive testing (CAT), and differential item functioning (DIF) analysis. | - *Item Response Theory for Psychologists* by Susan E. Embretson and Steven P. Reise<br>- *Educational Measurement* (4th Edition) edited by Robert L. Brennan<br>- [American Educational Research Association (AERA)](https://www.aera.net/) |
| Educational Data Mining and Learning Analytics | Utilizes digital trace data, predictive modeling, and process mining to identify behavioral learning trajectories and prevent student attrition. It equips educational data scientists to extract actionable insights from learning management systems and intelligent tutors. | - *The Handbook of Learning Analytics* (2nd Edition) edited by Charles Lang, George Siemens, Alyssa Friend Wise, and Dragan Gasevic<br>- [Society for Learning Analytics Research (SoLAR) Open Publications](https://www.solaresearch.org/) |
| Utilization-Focused and Mixed-Methods Evaluation | Teaches rigorous mixed-methods evaluation designs to determine whether, how, and why educational interventions achieve their intended systemic outcomes. This allows evaluators to produce actionable, stakeholder-responsive evidence for institutional reform. | - *Utilization-Focused Evaluation* by Michael Quinn Patton<br>- *Designing and Conducting Mixed Methods Research* by John W. Creswell and Vicki L. Plano Clark<br>- [BetterEvaluation Knowledge Platform](https://www.betterevaluation.org/) |
