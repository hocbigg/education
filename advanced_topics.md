# Advanced Topics

After finishing the core, review the two subsections and pick based on what appeals to you most:

- Choose **Design, Leadership, and Ethics** if you are drawn to planning learning experiences, reflecting on teaching practice, or thinking about moral questions in schools.
- Choose **Technology and Systems** if you are interested in digital tools, online teaching, or seeing education as interconnected systems.

## Research, Evidence, and Evaluation

| Subject | Why study? | Book / Text | Online Resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research Methods | It teaches how to investigate education questions systematically. You learn to collect and analyze data reliably. This builds skills to make evidence-based decisions in teaching. | [Creswell – *Educational Research*](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/educational-research/book258879) (Publisher previews) | [MIT OCW – Qualitative Methodology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/qualitative-research-methods/) |
| Measurement & Evaluation | It shows how to create and use tests that measure learning accurately. You gain tools to evaluate programs and student progress. This supports fair assessment and continuous improvement. | [Brennan – *Educational Measurement*](https://www.aera.net/Publications/Books/Educational-Measurement-4th-Edition) (Association previews) | [MIT OCW – Research Design](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/research-design/) |
| Program Evaluation | It provides ways to judge whether educational programs work well. You learn to assess effectiveness and suggest improvements. This helps you contribute to better school initiatives. | [Patton – *Evaluation*](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/practical-evaluation/book258880) (Publisher previews) | [BetterEvaluation.org](https://www.betterevaluation.org/) |

## Design, Leadership, and Ethics

| Subject | Why study? | Book / Text | Online Resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Curriculum Design | It teaches how to plan backward from goals to create meaningful units. You gain a framework to design lessons and courses that promote deep understanding. This makes your teaching more intentional and effective. | [Wiggins & McTighe – *Understanding by Design*](https://www.ascd.org/books/understanding-by-design) (ASCD previews) | [ASCD Webinars](https://www.ascd.org/webinars) |
| Teacher Professional Practice | It encourages ongoing reflection on your own teaching. You develop habits to learn from experience and grow professionally. This supports lifelong improvement as an educator. | [Schön – *Reflective Practitioner*](https://archive.org/details/reflectivepracti0000scho) | [Harvard Teaching Commons](https://teachingcommons.harvard.edu/) |
| Ethics in Education | It explores moral questions in teaching and school decisions. You learn to handle dilemmas with fairness and care. This helps you act responsibly toward students and colleagues. | [Strike – *Ethics and Education*](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/ethics-and-education/book258881) (Publisher previews) | [Open Yale – Philosophy](https://oyc.yale.edu/philosophy) |

## Technology and Systems

| Subject | Why study? | Book / Text | Online Resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Educational Technology | It examines how digital tools can enhance or change learning. You gain knowledge to choose and use technology thoughtfully. This prepares you for modern classrooms. | [Selwyn – *Learning Technologies*](https://www.routledge.com/Learning-Technologies/Selwyn/p/book/9781138024328) (Publisher previews) | [UCL IOE Lectures](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/ioe/) |
| Online & Blended Learning | It explains how to design effective digital and mixed courses. You learn principles of good online instruction. This helps you reach learners in flexible ways. | [Clark & Mayer – *E-Learning and the Science of Instruction*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/E+Learning+and+the+Science+of+Instruction%3A+Proven+Guidelines+for+Consumers+and+Designers+of+Multimedia+Learning%2C+4th+Edition-p-9781119158660) (Publisher previews) | [Open University](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) |
| Systems Thinking in Education | It teaches how to see education as connected parts (people, policies, resources). You develop ways to understand and improve whole systems. This supports solving complex school challenges. | [Meadows – *Thinking in Systems*](https://donellameadows.org/thinking-in-systems/) | [Donella Meadows Institute](https://donellameadows.org/) |
