Great Courses

This curated collection features standout university lecture series, full-length OpenCourseWare offerings, and high-impact online courses in Education. These resources move beyond standard introductory textbooks to explore cognitive learning architectures, school leadership models, educational policy debates, and digital pedagogical innovations.

  • The User-Friendly Classroom: Principles of Effective College Teaching (MIT OpenCourseWare / MIT / A. C. Kemp)
  • Teaching College-Level Science and Engineering (MIT OpenCourseWare / MIT / Janet Rankin)
  • Foundations of Teaching for Learning: Learners and Learning (Coursera / Commonwealth Education Trust / John MacBeath)
