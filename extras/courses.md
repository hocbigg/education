# Great Courses

This curated collection features standout university lecture series, full-length OpenCourseWare offerings, and high-impact online courses in Education. These resources move beyond standard introductory textbooks to explore cognitive learning architectures, school leadership models, educational policy debates, and digital pedagogical innovations.

## Learning Sciences, Psychology & Teaching Practice

- [Teaching & Learning in the Diverse Classroom (CornellX / Cornell University / Mathew L. Ouellett & Melina Ivanchikova)](https://www.edx.org/course/teaching-learning-in-the-diverse-classroom)
- [Inclusive Teaching: Supporting All Students in the College Classroom (ColumbiaX / Columbia University / Amanda Jungles & Chandani Patel)](https://www.edx.org/course/inclusive-teaching-supporting-all-students-in-the-college-classroom)
- [The User-Friendly Classroom: Principles of Effective College Teaching (MIT OpenCourseWare / MIT / A. C. Kemp)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-001-the-user-friendly-classroom-fall-2020/)
- [Teaching College-Level Science and Engineering (MIT OpenCourseWare / MIT / Janet Rankin)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-95j-teaching-college-level-science-and-engineering-fall-2015/)
- [Foundations of Teaching for Learning: Learners and Learning (Coursera / Commonwealth Education Trust / John MacBeath)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learning)
