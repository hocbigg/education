# Projects

This page is a curated directory of actionable project walkthroughs, step-by-step methodological guides, open templates, and authentic analytical workflows for education.

> *What I cannot create, I do not understand — Richard Feynman.*

Choose a real-world educational artifact you want to construct to discover and apply theoretical frameworks through hands-on practice.
